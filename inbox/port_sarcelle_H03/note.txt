DEVIATIONS FROM THE REFERENCE / APPROVED PLAN:
The ceremonial red portal is integrated into the building entrance landing. No boundary gate or enclosing walls are included.
The incense yard is reduced to the own entrance platform and covered porch. The incense-burner placeholder is inside the sanctuary.
The reference straight external stair becomes a return stair with wider clear landings and fully supported railing bases.
The upper foyer projects forward on the east side, permitting a direct stair arrival without a gallery obstructing the stair.
Full room layouts, kitchen, WCs, door openings and additional matching side/rear windows are supplied where the reference is silent.
The nave has a clerestory and double-height interior; the upper occupied rooms are in the rear annex.
Roof forms are closed supported assemblies with soffits, flashing and drainage. Exposed unfinished reinforcement is omitted.
Dragons, floral brackets, gilded panels and ridge ornaments are simplified sculptural geometry within the LOD0 budget.
The front sign uses raised fictional Latin lettering: TEMPLE DES MAREES PAISIBLES.
Overall projection grew slightly from the estimate to preserve clear circulation: still within the 18 x 19 m buildable area.
New tiles are 1254 px square, above the required 1024 minimum; 2048 px was preferred in the plan.
