Furniture style: creole_poor.
LOD0: 69,524 triangles total, including 7 working door leaves and 30 furniture placeholder boxes.
Units: metres. glTF +Y up; main facade +Z (source Z-up facade -Y). Origin: complete footprint centre at ground.
Main body 9.40 x 10.80 m including 2.40 m veranda; enclosed house 9.40 x 8.40 m.
Finished floor +1.80 m; 0.22 m floor/roof slabs; ceiling +4.58 m; roof cap +5.108 m; foundation -0.20 m.
Outer walls 0.30 m; partitions 0.12 m; hall 1.40 m clear; rooms 3.58 x 3.84 m.
Both stairs: 12 x 0.15 m risers, 11 x 0.32 m treads; slab width 1.40 m; clear width between rails approx 1.276 m.
Full landings; 1.0 m nominal continuous guards; roof is maintenance-only and is not a roof terrace.

DEVIATIONS FROM THE SINGLE REFERENCE:
Dimensions are inferred. A complete one-bedroom home, rear facade and side/rear windows were added for plausible use and cross ventilation.
Entrance leaves are closed for game animation. Both stairs, landings, door sizes and wall/slab depths are regularized for access.
A rear exit landing and stair were added; the raised plinth is non-habitable, and the veranda underside remains an open structural recess.
The solid flat roof has 2% rearward fall, a continuous soffit, metal edge caps, gutter, scupper, hollow swan-neck/downpipe and wall clips.
The drainage buildup makes the final height 5.108 m rather than the early approximate 4.95 m.
The hexagonal wire infill uses a simplified diamond wire section and approximately 160 mm cell diameter to meet the LOD0 budget.
Irregular blue-painted plinth stones and recessed white joints are modelled as geometry. Door/window frames are plain, lightly weathered white timber.
The planned hall picture was moved to the living room to keep the hall entirely 1.40 m clear; ceiling fans use low-profile housings.
People, plants, site/terrain, boundary objects, loose exterior furniture and real lettering are omitted.

CHECKS:
Geometry, metric UVs, floor/ceiling winding, true door openings, furniture containment/overlap, full door sweeps and exported glTF structure checked.
No LODs, collision meshes, proxies, cameras, lights or animation clips are included. Door motion is driven by the game.
No Unreal Engine runtime is present in this session; the checks cover the asset and glTF import, not an in-engine playtest.
