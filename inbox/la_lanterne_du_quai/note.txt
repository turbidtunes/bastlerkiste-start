Added full modelled service rooms, two WCs, two-bedroom owner flat, kitchen, bathroom and side/rear ventilation windows.
East stair and balcony void are set out to usable dimensions; balcony enlarged to 1.6 m projection and rails set to 1 m.
Added wall thickness, complete reveals, slab undersides, closed corrugated roof profile, supporting purlins and fully seated posts/column heads.
Roof column heads follow the roof pitch; exposed reinforcing reaches approximately 8 m maximum height, without creating another storey.
Added complete front gutter with swan-necks, wall-fixed downpipes, clips, entrance landings and steps.
Replaced reference lettering with fictional La Lanterne du Quai and Port Sarcelle signage.
Café tables use two chairs each (four total rather than eight planned) to retain a clear route from the entrance.
Bathroom leaf 12 uses R hinge, clearing the basin. Slightly narrowed bedroom dresser for bed-side access.
Generated tiles are 1254 px square (above the 1024 px minimum) rather than the preferred 2048 px.
Omitted reference street paving, outside chairs, plants, surrounding buildings and boundary elements.
