DEVIATIONS FROM REFERENCE / APPROVED PLAN:
The row is widened to the plot-specified four 8 m units; original four-colour order, shopfront rhythm, barred windows, balcony types and unfinished turquoise frame are retained.
Independent street stair entrances, shop pedestrian doors, rear exits, full room layouts and rear windows are supplied where the photograph is silent.
Side windows are omitted because both plot sides and intermediate walls are party walls. Front/rear windows and roof vent ducts supply the openings.
Balconies have real inward-opening doors; all guards/parapets are 1 m high. Exposed roof rods are outside circulation.
Turquoise upper stair has the same ascent direction as the lower flight and a second first-floor access door, preserving headroom.
Bathrooms are larger than the initial 1.8 x 2.2 m estimate; kitchen runs are 1.3 m and the fridge sits along the open-plan west wall to preserve access.
Entrance halls omit the proposed coat/shoe storage so the 1.4 m circulation remains clear. Bedroom wardrobes move to the west walls, away from windows and door swings.
Three maintenance hatches use separate sliding nodes; the turquoise terrace has a complete covered sitting-room placeholder set.
Complete drainage and wall-clipped downpipes are added. Simple shallow geometry represents render losses; fine weathering is in the material tiles.
Own supported entrance steps replace the photographed pavement transition; no surrounding paving, yards, wires or planting are delivered.
The roof frame, canopy and stairhead have real closed thickness and supports. Repeated grille/balustrade details are simplified within the LOD0 budget.
New tiles are 1254 px square, exceeding the required 1024 minimum; the plan preferred 2048.
