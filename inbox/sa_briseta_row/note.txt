SIZE / ACCESS

Exterior and shared party walls 0.30 m; partitions 0.12 m, with short embedded construction joints. Party walls, including the left end, have no windows as required by the plot.
Each home has its own U stair: 18 risers per storey, 172.22 mm rise, 280 mm going, 18 mm projecting nosings with 5 mm edge chamfers. Treads 1.25 m wide, with at least 1.20 m clear between handrails; landings 1.40 m deep. Rear landings are bounded by full-height walls. Circulation corridors are at least 1.40 m wide. Four first-floor balconies have approximately 1.4 m clear depth, door access and continuous iron guards. Pitched roofs are unoccupied maintenance surfaces; there is no roof terrace or occupied attic.
A–C: ground living/dining + kitchen + bathroom + hall/stair; first two bedrooms + bathroom + hall/stair; second main bedroom + office/sewing room + bathroom + landing.
D: ground grocery + stockroom + staff WC + private hall/stair; first living/dining + kitchen + bathroom + hall/stair; second two bedrooms + bathroom + landing. Separate side entrance reaches the private stair without entering the grocery. The staff WC is behind a 1.44 m cross passage.
Bathrooms have roof-linked ventilation; D ground WC has a short ceiling-level branch to the shaft. Habitable rooms have front, rear and/or corner-side windows.

DEVIATIONS FROM THE REFERENCE / FINAL PLAN ADJUSTMENTS
1. The four fronts are widened to 7 m each to match the supplied plots; the reference has narrower proportions. Its white/ochre/rose/cream sequence, shutters, stone surrounds, timber shopfront, iron balconies, clay tiles and lanterns are retained.
2. Consistent floor levels and aligned eaves replace the small height differences between the reference houses. The roof is a continuous low-pitch tiled construction with the exposed right-hand end hipped; party divisions remain legible in the facades.
3. Balconies are deeper for usable access and guarded returns. Enclosed depth was reduced from the planned 9.4 m to 9.2 m to fit rear entrance treads inside the 11 m envelope. No rear low annex is added.
4. Interiors, stairs, bathrooms, rear openings and extraction shafts are invented because the reference does not show them. Additional shuttered ground-floor front windows light A–C. Party walls stay blind under the plot exception.
5. The corner owner gets a separate side entrance. Its staff WC is moved back to provide a private cross passage; the rear stockroom remains at least 2.4 m deep.
6. Doors and window shutters are delivered closed. Slim fixed shutter details beside balcony doors preserve the motif while leaving balcony access clear.
7. Each living room has a four-seat open dining area; its cabinet and seating are arranged around a clear route. Kitchen tables are compact two-seat breakfast tables. All furniture is boxes, including one gun cabinet per home, as requested.
8. Bathroom wall finishes are full-height white glazed tile. Chimney positions follow the invented ventilation shafts; stack heights follow their local roof slopes.
9. Shop and street lettering are fictional. Vegetation, yards, roads, paving beyond the attached entrance steps/landings, boundary structures, vehicles, people and exterior loose props are omitted.
